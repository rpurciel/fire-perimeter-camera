1 · Landmarks (optional) ·

A landmark is a feature you can find both in a frame and on the map: a summit, a tower, a road junction, a lake shore. Each one pins the calibration where the skyline alone is weak, above all the direction (pan). Landmarks on the stills matter most: they set the offset of that camera position.

      2 · Calibrate

      km

      Puts the modelled skyline on the silhouette edges of the panorama frames and of the camera's stills (no detected skyline is used), pulled toward the landmarks. The shared correction comes from the panorama; each camera position seen in the stills then gets its own small pan/tilt offset. Takes about a minute per camera. The result is a proposal: check it in step 3, then accept it.
