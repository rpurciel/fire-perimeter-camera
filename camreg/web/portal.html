      Elevation model (DEM)

      The terrain the cameras are modelled on: the skyline the calibration fits, and the ground the fire edges are draped on. SRTM (30 m tiles, in the DEM folder) smooths summits and ridges away. USGS 3DEP (about 10 m, downloaded from the USGS for an area around the cameras; SRTM beyond it) resolves them. With 3DEP, the camera's site and height must be right to a few metres: use Check site on each camera.

      Changing the DEM unloads the cameras (Load cameras again). A calibration made on the other DEM is marked; calibrate the camera again in the calibration step.
